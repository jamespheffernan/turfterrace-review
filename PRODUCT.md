# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

adaptive

## Users

James uses Turf Review on iPhone, iPad, and Mac to read and decide on work prepared by agents.

## Product Purpose

Present a review queue, readable documents, annotations, explicit decisions, and notifications that open the exact review.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Native SwiftUI navigation surrounds HTML documents. Hosted content uses authenticated sessions. Decisions require server confirmation. Context audio exists for some documents; hosted full-document audio is not yet available. Preserve login, reading position, annotation, and decision behavior.

## Product Principles

- Download the library so opening saved work does not require a network round trip.
- Prioritize pending work during the first download.
- Keep source content intact and keep controls secondary to reading.
- Distinguish downloaded, downloading, and failed states truthfully.

## Open decisions

A visual reference was requested; no reference has yet been supplied. The implementation will use native reading conventions unless James steers it.
