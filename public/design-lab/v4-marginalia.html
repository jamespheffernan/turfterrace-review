KitchenLux Q2 Campaign Brief

Campaign Overview

The Q2 campaign will focus on our premium outdoor kitchen line, targeting homeowners who are actively planning backyard renovations. We'll emphasize the durability and design-forward aesthetics that differentiate KitchenLux from commodity alternatives.

"Every great outdoor kitchen starts with a conversation about how people actually live in their backyards." — internal positioning doc

Target Audience

Primary: homeowners aged 35-55 with household income above $150k, located in Sun Belt states. Secondary: landscape architects and high-end contractors who spec products for clients.

Key Segments

  • The Entertainer — hosts regularly, wants a showpiece
  • The Chef — serious about cooking, needs professional-grade
