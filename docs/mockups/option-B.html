Migrate the ingest tier to the new queue
Drafted by the platform pod · 1,840 words · 6 review items

The current ingest path routes every event through a single synchronous worker pool. Under the Q2 peak it sat at 94% saturation for nine hours and dropped 0.3% of events on the floor — small, but the wrong events.

This plan moves ingest behind a durable queue so spikes buffer instead of drop, and so we can replay a bad window without a redeploy.

Item 2. Cut over writes in a single switch rather than a dual-write window?
✓ Yes
✗ No

The team prefers a hard cutover at low traffic with a tested rollback, arguing dual-write doubles the surface for subtle drift.

Rollback & blast radius

A failed cutover reverts in under four minutes by flipping the router flag. The queue retains 24h, so no window is unrecoverable.
